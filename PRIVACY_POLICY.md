# Privacy Policy for USSD Code Manager

## Last Updated: April 17, 2025

Thank you for using our USSD Code Manager app. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

## Information We Collect

Our app collects the following information:

- **Phone State and Phone Numbers**: We request access to your phone state and phone numbers solely to execute USSD codes directly from the app. This information is never stored or transmitted outside your device.
- **Call Phone Permission**: This permission is required to dial USSD codes directly from the app.
- **Network State**: We check your network state to ensure USSD codes can be executed properly.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:
- Execute USSD codes when you request them
- Provide emergency service access when needed
- Improve the app's functionality and user experience

## Data Storage and Security

All data is stored locally on your device. We do not:
- Store your data on external servers
- Share your information with third parties
- Track your usage beyond what's necessary for app functionality

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Decline permissions (though this may limit app functionality)
- Request information about what data the app has stored locally
- Delete all app data by uninstalling the app

## Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
